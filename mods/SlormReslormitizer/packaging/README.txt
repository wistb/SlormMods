SlormReslormitizer
Operates Rick Manalan's Slormitizer in The Slormancer: loads the
cheapest Slormandrites for the odds you ask for, and reinforces.

REQUIREMENTS
  The Slormancer on Steam (Windows, 64-bit)
  The Aurie mod loader, installed first. The easiest way is SlormAPI's
  installer: https://github.com/tsotsas/SlormAPI/releases
  Microsoft Visual C++ 2015-2022 Redistributable (x64). Most PCs already
  have it. If the mod does not load, install it from
  https://aka.ms/vs/17/release/vc_redist.x64.exe

INSTALL
  1. Back up your save: %LocalAppData%\The_Slormancer
  2. Open the game folder
     (Steam: right-click the game > Manage > Browse local files)
  3. Extract this zip into it, so SlormReslormitizer.dll lands in
     mods\Aurie
  4. Start the game from Steam as usual

USE
  Open the Slormitizer at Rick Manalan and put an item in it. The
  SlormReslormitizer window shows by itself there; F8 hides or shows
  it, and the key can be changed on the Options tab.

  Targets     The chance of success, Critical Reinforce and Double
              Reinforce to reach at least, and the Failure to stay
              under. The plan is the cheapest mix of Slormandrites of
              Fate, Aptitude, Virtue and Harmony in your stock that
              gets there.
  Slormite    Use whichever slormite is in the Slormitizer, or choose
              one. More are loaded when it runs out, and lower
              slormites can be merged up into the one in use.
  Load        Puts the plan in the Slormitizer. Nothing is spent.
  Reinforce   Loads the plan and activates the Slormitizer once.
  once
  Repeat      Keeps reinforcing until the item reaches the level you
              set, or the targets can no longer be met.

  Nothing is activated unless the numbers the game itself shows meet
  your targets.

UNINSTALL
  Delete mods\Aurie\SlormReslormitizer.dll and
  SlormReslormitizer-README.txt from the game folder; your settings
  are the other SlormReslormitizer files in mods\Aurie. The game's own
  files are never changed.
